# Project Tomo — Design Context

## Users

People who value deep, intentional relationships and have a sentimental streak — they're the ones who keep letters, remember the small details, and want a private space to hold onto the stories that matter. They're not tracking contacts for networking; they're preserving memories of people they love. The app is a personal memory box, not a productivity tool.

## Brand Personality

**Warm · Crafted · Playful**

Tomo feels like a beautifully made object you carry with you — handcrafted but not fussy, personal but not precious. It has a quiet warmth that invites you in, the kind of care you feel when something has been made with intention. There's a subtle playfulness — little moments of magic and delight — but it never tries too hard. The name "Tomo" (友, friend) sets the tone: familiar, close, human.

**Emotional goals:** Opening Tomo should feel like opening a box of letters from loved ones — warm and slightly magical. The confidence that you truly know the people you care about, paired with delight in rediscovering small moments.

## Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone:** Warm minimalism with craft details. Paper-like textures, generous whitespace, soft shadows. The interface should feel tactile and analog — a digital object that still evokes physical warmth.

**References:**
- **Arc Browser** — polished, delightful micro-interactions, a sense of joy in every gesture
- **Bear** — warm typography, minimal chrome, content-first with personality
- **Things 3** — meticulous craft in every pixel, subtle joy in completing actions, restraint that feels intentional

**Anti-references:**
- Corporate/enterprise CRM tools (Salesforce, HubSpot) — Tomo is not a contact manager
- Social media (Instagram, Facebook) — no feeds, no likes, no public profiles
- Gamified apps (Duolingo, streaks) — no badges, no engagement pressure, no guilt mechanics

**Theme:** Adaptive light/dark. Light mode is the primary experience — warm ivory paper with terracotta accents. Dark mode is equally warm, using deep charcoals rather than pure blacks.

## Typography

| Role | Spec |
|------|------|
| Title (wordmark) | Lora Bold, 28pt — the one serif moment, gives warmth and identity |
| Names / headings | System Rounded, 17pt semibold |
| Body | System Rounded, 15pt regular |
| Caption | System Rounded, 11pt regular |
| Countdown | System Rounded, 28pt regular |
| Emphasis | System Rounded, 15pt semibold |

The rounded system font is deliberate — it softens the interface without being childish, matching the warm-but-not-precious personality.

Layout uses a two-column masonry (waterfall) grid. Cards have generous padding and soft warm shadows. Glass effects (iOS 26) on bottom bars and chrome elements.

## Motion

Dominant spring animation: **0.35s duration**. Shorter springs (0.25–0.3s) for micro-interactions. Transitions use push/move with opacity for a sense of physical space. Motion should feel organic and unhurried — things settle into place rather than snap.

## Design Principles

1. **Memory over management** — Every decision should serve the act of remembering, not organizing. If a feature feels like work, it doesn't belong.

2. **Warmth in every pixel** — From the ivory paper background to the terracotta accents to the rounded type, the app should feel like it was made by a person who cares, for people who care.

3. **Quiet magic** — The AI features (entity detection, smart extraction) should feel like gentle surprises, not technology demos. Magic happens in the background and reveals itself softly.

4. **Craft over cleverness** — Prefer meticulous, simple solutions over flashy ones. A perfectly tuned spring animation is worth more than a particle effect. Restraint is a feature.

5. **Private and personal** — This is not social. No sharing, no feeds, no metrics. The app exists in the intimate space between you and the people you love.
